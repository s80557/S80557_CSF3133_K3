About the Event

Join us for an intensive full-day hands-on programming workshop designed to
equip students with foundational and practical web development skills.

Instructor: Prof. Ts. Ariyssa Irdina

Prof. Ts. Ariyssa has over 10 years of experience teaching in web development
and will guide students through the coding practices.

Activity: Live Coding & Portfolio Project

Participants will work on a real-time hands-on exercise to construct and publish
their personal portfolio landing page by the end of the day.
